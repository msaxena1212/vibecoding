Privacy Policy

Your data, our commitment. Transparency in how we handle your information.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how POSify ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when using our Point-of-Sale (POS) web application. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring the security of your data. This policy applies to all features and services offered by POSify.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Business Data: Information related to your business operations, including product details, inventory levels, sales transactions, customer purchase history, employee data, and pricing information. This data is essential for the core functionality of our POS system.
  • Account Information: Your name, email address, company name, and password used to create and manage your POSify account.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our application, such as features used, pages visited, and time spent on the platform. This helps us improve user experience and identify areas for development.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser type, device information, and operating system details, collected for security, performance monitoring, and troubleshooting.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our POS web application and its features.
  • To process your sales transactions, manage inventory, and facilitate customer management.
  • To personalize your experience and provide tailored content and features.
  • To communicate with you, including responding to inquiries, sending service-related notices, and providing updates.
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve our application's performance, functionality, and user interface.
  • To ensure the security and integrity of our application and your data, preventing fraud and unauthorized access.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

4. Data Protection and Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption: Sensitive data is encrypted both in transit (using TLS/SSL) and at rest.
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls are in place to ensure only authorized personnel can access your data.
  • Regular Audits: We conduct regular security assessments and audits to identify and address potential vulnerabilities.
  • Secure Infrastructure: Our application is hosted on secure, reliable cloud infrastructure with comprehensive security protocols.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of data protection.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (e.g., payment gateways, analytics providers) to enhance our application's functionality. These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. We only share information with these third parties as necessary to provide our services and ensure they adhere to strict confidentiality and security standards.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. You can manage your account settings directly within the POS application. If you have questions or requests regarding your data, please contact us using the information provided below.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
support@posify.com
Or via our Contact Page.
