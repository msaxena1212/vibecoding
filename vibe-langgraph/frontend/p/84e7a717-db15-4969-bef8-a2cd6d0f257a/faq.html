Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our data dashboard platform.

General Questions

What is a data dashboard?

A data dashboard is a visual representation of key performance indicators (KPIs) and other important metrics, allowing you to monitor and analyze your data at a glance.

How do I create a dashboard?

Our platform provides an intuitive drag-and-drop interface. Simply select your data sources, choose your chart types, and customize the layout to create your perfect dashboard.

Technical Questions

What data sources are supported?

We support a wide range of data sources, including CSV files, databases (SQL, NoSQL), and various APIs.

What chart types are available?

We offer a comprehensive chart library, including line graphs, bar charts, pie charts, scatter plots, and more.

Account Questions

How do I sign up for a free trial?

You can sign up for a free trial on our homepage. Simply click the "Start Your Free Trial" button and follow the instructions.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings.
