Frequently Asked Questions

What type of photography do you specialize in?

I specialize in landscape, portrait, and urban photography. I love capturing the beauty of the natural world, the essence of individuals, and the vibrant energy of cityscapes.

How can I book a photoshoot?

You can book a photoshoot by visiting the Contact page and filling out the inquiry form. Please provide details about the type of photoshoot you're interested in, your desired date and location, and any specific requirements you may have.

What is your pricing for photography services?

Pricing varies depending on the type of photography service, the duration of the shoot, and any additional requirements. Please contact me with details about your project, and I will provide you with a customized quote.

What is your editing process?

My editing process involves careful attention to detail to enhance the natural beauty of each image. I use industry-standard software to adjust colors, tones, and sharpness while maintaining a realistic and authentic look.

Do you provide prints or digital files?

Yes, I provide both prints and high-resolution digital files. You can choose to order prints directly from me, or you can purchase the digital files and have them printed at a lab of your choice.

What rights do I have to the photos?

Generally, you'll receive personal usage rights for the photos, allowing you to share them with friends and family, and use them for personal projects. Commercial usage rights may require a separate agreement. Details are outlined in the contract provided before the photoshoot.
