Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nova Horizon?

Nova Horizon is a pioneering space tourism company offering meticulously crafted journeys beyond Earth, blending cutting-edge technology with unparalleled luxury and safety.

How safe are your spaceflights?

Your safety is our paramount concern. We utilize state-of-the-art spacecraft, rigorously tested and maintained to the highest aerospace standards. Our crew undergoes extensive training, exceeding industry requirements.

What is included in the price of a journey?

Our journeys include comprehensive pre-flight training, luxurious accommodations, gourmet meals prepared by world-renowned chefs, personalized concierge service, and of course, the unforgettable experience of spaceflight itself.

What kind of training is provided before the flight?

We provide extensive pre-flight training, including simulations, centrifuge training, and survival courses, to ensure you are fully prepared for the unique environment of space.

Are there any age or health restrictions for participants?

Participants must be at least 18 years of age and meet certain health requirements. A comprehensive medical evaluation is required before booking a flight.
