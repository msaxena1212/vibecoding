Frequently Asked Questions

Nova Horizon prioritizes passenger safety above all else. Our spacecraft undergo rigorous testing and maintenance. Our crew is highly trained in emergency procedures. We provide comprehensive pre-flight training to prepare passengers for the space environment. Additionally, our vessels are equipped with advanced life support systems and emergency protocols.

To book a space tourism package, please contact our concierge service to discuss your preferences and requirements. A consultation will be scheduled to review available packages, flight schedules, and pre-flight training. Upon selection, a deposit is required to secure your booking. Our team will guide you through the necessary medical evaluations and documentation.

All passengers undergo comprehensive pre-flight training tailored to their chosen package. This includes simulations of the space environment, safety protocols, and emergency procedures. You'll learn about the effects of g-forces, weightlessness, and radiation exposure. Our expert trainers will ensure you are well-prepared for a safe and enjoyable journey.

Our orbital flights offer luxurious accommodations designed for comfort and relaxation. Each passenger enjoys a private cabin equipped with a personal entertainment system, gourmet meals, and panoramic views of Earth. Our dedicated crew provides personalized service to ensure your needs are met. Cabins are designed to minimize the effects of weightlessness and maximize comfort.

While we welcome passengers of all ages, certain health restrictions apply to ensure passenger safety. A comprehensive medical evaluation is required to assess your fitness for space travel. Pre-existing conditions such as heart disease, uncontrolled diabetes, or severe respiratory issues may disqualify you from participation. Our medical team will review your health records and advise accordingly.
