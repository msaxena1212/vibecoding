Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Guardian Angel.

General Questions

What is Guardian Angel?

Guardian Angel is a simple app designed to ensure your well-being through daily check-ins. It automatically alerts your trusted contacts if you need help.

How does the check-in system work?

You simply tap a button each day to confirm you're okay. You can set personalized reminders to help you remember.

Privacy & Security

How is my data protected?

Your data is encrypted and protected. We collect minimal information, focusing solely on your safety.

What information do you collect?

We collect minimal information necessary for the app to function, such as your check-in times and trusted contacts.

Technical Questions

How much battery does the app use?

Guardian Angel is designed for minimal impact, using very little battery and data.

Does the app require a constant internet connection?

The app requires an internet connection to send alerts to your trusted contacts if you miss a check-in.

Emergency Alerts

What happens if I miss a check-in?

If you miss your check-ins, your trusted contacts are automatically notified with your last known activity.

How quickly are my contacts notified?

Your contacts are notified shortly after you miss your scheduled check-in time, ensuring a quick response.
