Stay Safe, Effortlessly: Core Features

SoloProtect is designed to be your reliable safety companion. Here's how it works:

  • One-Tap Check-in: A single tap confirms you're okay each day.
  • Automated Alerts: If you miss check-ins for a defined period, your emergency contact is automatically notified.
  • Emergency Contact Management: Easily add and manage trusted contacts.
  • Customizable Reminders: Set reminders to ensure you never forget to check in.
  • Optional Health/Mood Notes: Keep a private log of your well-being.
  • Clear Escalation Flow: Know exactly what happens in an emergency.
