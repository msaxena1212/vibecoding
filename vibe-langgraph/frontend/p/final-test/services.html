Our Services

Explore the ultimate frontier with our curated luxury space tourism packages. From lunar flybys to residential orbital stays.

The Overview Effect: A Transformative Perspective

Imagine gazing back at Earth from the inky blackness of space. This is the Overview Effect—a profound cognitive shift experienced by astronauts, a feeling of interconnectedness and a renewed appreciation for our planet. Nova Horizon offers you the opportunity to experience this life-altering perspective firsthand. Our curated journeys are designed to ignite your curiosity, expand your understanding, and leave you with a lasting sense of wonder. Prepare to be transformed.

Lunar Voyager: Witness Earthrise

Embark on the Lunar Voyager, a breathtaking 7-day journey to the Moon. This is more than a trip; it's a pilgrimage to witness the awe-inspiring Earthrise from the lunar surface. Relax in your luxury space suite, savor gourmet zero-G dining, and receive personalized astronaut training to fully immerse yourself in the experience. Our exclusive lunar viewing experience will leave you speechless as you witness our planet in a new light. Price: $750,000.

Orbital Escape: Your Luxury Hotel in the Stars

Escape the ordinary with a 14-day stay in our luxury orbital hotel. Experience unparalleled comfort and service while orbiting Earth. Enjoy spacious orbital suites, world-class cuisine, and a personalized exercise program to maintain your well-being. Our state-of-the-art stargazing observatory offers breathtaking views of the cosmos. Immerse yourself in the Overview Effect from the comfort of your private sanctuary. Price: $1,200,000.

Cosmic Odyssey: Journey to the Edge of the Solar System

For the ultimate space adventure, embark on the Cosmic Odyssey—a 30-day journey to the edge of our solar system. Witness the beauty of nebulae and distant galaxies in unparalleled detail. This exclusive experience includes a private research lab, advanced life support systems, and personalized scientific mentorship from leading astrophysicists. Explore the cosmos and unlock the secrets of the universe. Price: $5,000,000.
