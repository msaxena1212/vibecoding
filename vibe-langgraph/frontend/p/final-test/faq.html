Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Overview Effect? +
The Overview Effect is a cognitive shift in awareness reported by astronauts when viewing Earth from space. It often leads to a feeling of interconnectedness and a renewed appreciation for our planet.
What types of space tourism packages does Nova Horizon offer? +
Nova Horizon offers a range of luxury space tourism packages, including lunar flybys, orbital stays in our luxury hotel, and journeys to the edge of our solar system. Each package includes personalized astronaut training, gourmet dining, and exclusive viewing experiences.
How much does it cost to travel with Nova Horizon? +
The cost of our space tourism packages varies depending on the destination and duration of the journey. Prices range from $750,000 for a lunar flyby to $5,000,000 for a journey to the edge of our solar system.
What safety measures are in place for space travel? +
Safety is our top priority at Nova Horizon. We adhere to the highest safety standards and work with experienced aerospace engineers and medical professionals to ensure the well-being of our passengers. Our spacecraft undergo rigorous testing and are equipped with advanced life support systems.
