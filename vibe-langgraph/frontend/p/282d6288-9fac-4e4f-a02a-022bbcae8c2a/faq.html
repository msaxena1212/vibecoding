Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What types of pens and pencils do you offer?

We offer a wide range of pens, including ballpoint, gel, fountain, and rollerball pens. Our pencil selection includes graphite, mechanical, and colored pencils.

Yes, we ship internationally. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on the destination.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unused items in their original packaging. Please see our Terms and Conditions for more details.

Product Specific Questions

How do I refill my fountain pen?

Fountain pens can be refilled using ink cartridges or a converter. Instructions for refilling your specific model can be found in the product manual or on our website's Support page.

What is the difference between graphite grades?

Graphite grades range from hard (H) to soft (B). Harder grades produce lighter lines, while softer grades produce darker lines. The HB grade is a common middle ground.

Order & Shipping Questions

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your order on the carrier's website.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay.
