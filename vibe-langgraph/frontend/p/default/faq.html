General Information

What is space tourism?

Space tourism involves commercial travel to space for recreational, leisure, or business purposes. Nova Horizon offers exclusive journeys to experience the wonder of space.

What makes Nova Horizon different?

Nova Horizon provides a luxurious and safe space tourism experience, blending cutting-edge technology with personalized service. Our team of experts ensures every detail is meticulously planned and executed.

Safety & Training

How safe is space tourism?

Safety is our top priority. Nova Horizon adheres to the highest safety standards, with rigorous testing, constant maintenance, and a team of experienced professionals overseeing every mission.

What kind of training is required?

Passengers undergo comprehensive pre-flight training, including medical evaluations, simulator sessions, and emergency procedure drills. Our training ensures you are prepared for the unique environment of space.

Pricing & Booking

How much does a space tourism trip cost?

Pricing varies depending on the destination, duration, and level of customization. Please contact our concierge team for detailed pricing information and available packages.

How do I book a voyage?

To book a voyage, schedule a consultation with our team. We will discuss your preferences, answer your questions, and guide you through the booking process.

Voyage Details

What is included in the Lunar Escape package?

The Lunar Escape package includes a breathtaking orbit around the Moon, private cabin accommodations, gourmet dining, and unparalleled views of the Earth and the lunar landscape.

What should I bring on my voyage?

We provide a detailed packing list before your departure, but essential items include comfortable clothing, personal toiletries, and any necessary medications. Cameras and other recording devices are highly encouraged!
