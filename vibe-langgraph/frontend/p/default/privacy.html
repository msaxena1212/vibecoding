Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to Nova Horizon. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Data Collection

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, book a journey, contact us, or fill out a form. This information may include your name, email address, phone number, payment information, and travel preferences.

We also collect certain information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, and browsing behavior on our website. This data helps us improve our services and personalize your experience.

Use of Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Process your bookings and payments
  • Communicate with you about your journeys
  • Personalize your experience on our website
  • Send you marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations

Cookie Policy

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and collect information about how you use our website. Cookies are small data files stored on your device. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

GDPR Compliance

Nova Horizon is committed to complying with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal information. You also have the right to object to the processing of your personal information and to withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise your GDPR rights, please contact us at privacy@novahorizon.com.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. These measures include encryption, firewalls, and access controls.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to process payments, analyze data, and send marketing communications. These services have their own privacy policies, which you should review.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on our website and notify you by email if the changes are significant.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@novahorizon.com.
