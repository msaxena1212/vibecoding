Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ISRO's missions, programs, and services.

General Questions

What is ISRO?

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is the national space agency of India, responsible for space-based operations, space exploration, and the development of space technologies.

What is ISRO's mission?

ISRO's mission is to advance space technology and leverage its applications for the nation's development.

Missions & Programs

What is Chandrayaan-3?

Chandrayaan-3 is India's third lunar exploration mission, which successfully landed on the moon's south pole.

What is Mangalyaan?

Mangalyaan, also known as the Mars Orbiter Mission, was India's first interplanetary mission, successfully placed into orbit around Mars.

What is Aditya-L1?

Aditya-L1 is India's first solar mission to study the Sun, placed in a halo orbit around the Lagrange point 1 (L1) of the Sun-Earth system.

What is Gaganyaan?

Gaganyaan is India's human spaceflight program, aiming to send Indian astronauts into space.

Launch Vehicles

What is PSLV?

PSLV (Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle) is a reliable and versatile launch vehicle used for placing satellites into polar and low Earth orbits.

What is GSLV?

GSLV (Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle) is a launch vehicle for placing satellites into geostationary orbit.

Navigation Systems

What is GAGAN?

GAGAN (GPS Aided GEO Augmented Navigation) is a satellite-based augmentation system for air navigation.

What is NavIC?

NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation) is an independent regional navigation satellite system designed to provide accurate positioning information service to users in India and the surrounding region.
