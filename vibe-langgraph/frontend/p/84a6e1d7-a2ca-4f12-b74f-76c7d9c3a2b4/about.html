About ISRO

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is the national space agency of India, headquartered in Bangalore. It operates under the Department of Space (DOS) which is directly overseen by the Prime Minister of India.

History

ISRO was formed in 1969, with the vision of harnessing space technology for national development. Since then, it has achieved significant milestones in space exploration and satellite technology.

Key Milestones

  • 1975: Launch of India's first satellite, Aryabhata.
  • 2008: Successful launch of Chandrayaan-1, India's first lunar probe.
  • 2014: Successful Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan).
  • 2017: Launch of 104 satellites in a single mission.
  • 2019: Chandrayaan-2 mission.
  • 2023: Successful landing of Chandrayaan-3 on the Moon's South Pole.
  • 2023: Launch of Aditya-L1, India's first solar mission.

Mission and Values

ISRO's mission is to advance space technology and leverage its applications for the nation's development. Our core values include:

  • Commitment to scientific excellence.
  • Dedication to national development.
  • Innovation and continuous improvement.
  • Transparency and accountability.
