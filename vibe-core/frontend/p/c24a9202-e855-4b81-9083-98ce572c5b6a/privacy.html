Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Guardian Angel ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Information We Collect

  • Location Data: We collect your device's location to provide emergency alerts to your trusted contacts if you miss your check-ins. This data is only used when necessary to ensure your safety.
  • Check-in Data: We store your check-in history to monitor your well-being and trigger alerts if you fail to check in.
  • Contact Information: You provide contact information for your trusted contacts so we can notify them in case of an emergency.
  • Device Information: We collect device information such as device type and operating system to optimize the App's performance.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide the core functionality of the App, including daily check-ins and emergency alerts.
  • To improve the App's performance and user experience.
  • To communicate with you about updates and important information.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Your data is encrypted and stored securely.

Sharing Your Information

We only share your information with your trusted contacts when you miss your check-ins and an emergency alert is triggered. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can manage your trusted contacts and check-in settings within the App.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@guardianangel.com.
