Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Supermarket POS software.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and business details.
  • Transaction data, including sales records, payment information, and product details.
  • Usage data, such as how you interact with our software and which features you use.
  • Device information, including your IP address, browser type, and operating system.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our software.
  • Process transactions and manage your account.
  • Communicate with you about updates, promotions, and support.
  • Analyze usage trends and optimize our services.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. These measures include encryption, firewalls, and regular security audits.

5. Data Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist us with payment processing, data analytics, and customer support.
  • Legal authorities if required by law.
  • Third parties with your consent.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your information.
  • Correct inaccuracies in your information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Object to the processing of your information.

7. Cookies

We use cookies to improve your experience and personalize content. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@example.com.
