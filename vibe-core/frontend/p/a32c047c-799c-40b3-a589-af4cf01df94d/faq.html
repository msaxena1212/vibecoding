Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is SoloProtect and how does it work?

SoloProtect is a safety app designed to provide peace of mind. It allows you to check in daily, and if you miss your check-in for a specified period, your emergency contact will be automatically notified.

How do I add or manage emergency contacts?

You can easily add and manage trusted contacts within the app's settings. Simply navigate to the "Emergency Contacts" section and follow the instructions.

Privacy and Security

What data does SoloProtect collect?

SoloProtect collects minimal personal information, primarily your name, contact information, and the details of your emergency contacts. We do not track your location unless an alert is triggered.

How is my data protected?

We use secure data encryption to protect your data and are committed to transparency and responsible data handling. Your privacy is our priority.

Emergency Procedures

What happens if I miss a check-in?

If you miss your check-in for a defined period, your emergency contact will be automatically notified via SMS, email, and in-app notification.

How quickly are emergency contacts alerted?

Emergency contacts are alerted immediately after the defined period of missed check-ins has elapsed. We use multiple channels to ensure swift notification.
