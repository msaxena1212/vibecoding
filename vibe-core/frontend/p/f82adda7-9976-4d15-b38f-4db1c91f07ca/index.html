Effortless Sleep Tracking

Automatically record your sleep duration, stages (wake, light, deep, REM), and sleep efficiency. No complicated setup, just restful nights and insightful data. We use the latest in sensor technology, including support for actigraphy, to provide the most accurate data possible.

Visualize Your Sleep Journey

Explore interactive charts and graphs that reveal your unique sleep patterns. Easily identify trends, pinpoint disturbances, and gain a deeper understanding of your sleep health. Our visualizations are powered by libraries like Chart.js and D3.js, ensuring a smooth and intuitive experience.

Personalized Insights for Better Sleep

Receive tailored recommendations based on your sleep data. Optimize your bedtime routine, identify potential sleep disruptors, and unlock strategies for achieving truly restorative sleep. Our analysis is informed by established sleep research and best practices, ensuring you receive actionable advice.

Seamless Integrations

Connect with your favorite health and fitness apps via Health Connect API and HealthKit. Import and export your sleep data for a holistic view of your well-being. We support integrations with devices like Fitbit and Oura Ring, allowing you to consolidate your health data in one place.

Science-Backed Accuracy

Our sleep tracking algorithms are based on the gold standard of sleep analysis, polysomnography (PSG), and validated against actigraphy data. We leverage libraries like MNE-sleep and sleePy to ensure the accuracy and reliability of your sleep data. While consumer sleep trackers can't replace a clinical sleep study, we strive to provide the most scientifically sound insights possible.
