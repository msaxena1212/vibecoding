End-to-End Encryption: Your Conversations, Your Privacy.

Your privacy matters. That's why we use the Signal Protocol, renowned for its robust end-to-end encryption. Every message, every call, every file you share is protected from prying eyes. Perfect Forward Secrecy ensures that even if a key is compromised, your past communications remain secure. Communicate with confidence, knowing your data is safe.

Enjoy the flexibility of a Progressive Web App (PWA). Access our messaging service on any device, from desktops to mobile phones, without the hassle of app store downloads.
