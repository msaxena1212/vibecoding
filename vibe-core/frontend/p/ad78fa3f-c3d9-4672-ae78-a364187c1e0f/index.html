Gauging India's Emission Landscape

India, a rapidly developing nation, faces significant environmental challenges. Our platform provides a comprehensive, data-driven approach to understanding the complex web of gas emissions impacting the country. From the dominant CO2 contributions of our energy sector to the critical air pollutants like PM2.5 and NOx affecting urban health, we break down the data. Explore national totals, regional variations, and sector-specific impacts, visualized through interactive charts and detailed reports. We leverage data from trusted sources such as the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), and international databases like EDGAR and Climate Watch (WRI) to offer an unparalleled view of emission trends.

Key Emission Sources in India

Energy

Dominant CO2 from coal-fired power plants and industrial energy consumption.

Transport

Road vehicles, aviation, and shipping contribute NOx, PM2.5, and CO2.

Industry

Manufacturing, cement, and steel production emit SO2, NOx, and GHGs.

Agriculture

Livestock, rice cultivation, and fertilizers release significant Methane (CH4) and N2O.
