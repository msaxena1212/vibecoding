Our Data Sources & Methodology

We are committed to data integrity and transparency. Understand how we collect, process, and present emission data for India.

Data Aggregation & Calculation

Trusted Data Origins

We meticulously source our data from reputable government agencies like the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC). Complementing this, we integrate data from globally recognized databases such as EDGAR and Climate Watch (WRI) for a comprehensive view.

Where direct real-time data is limited, we employ scientifically validated emission factors and models, adapting established methodologies like those from the IPCC to the Indian context.

View All Sources

Rigorous Calculation Process

Our process involves consolidating information from multiple sources, utilizing a curated database of emission factors specific to Indian sources (vehicles, industries, fuels). Advanced analytics, employing statistical models and libraries like Pandas and NumPy, are used to analyze trends and generate insights.

We clearly outline our calculation processes and any limitations, ensuring users understand how our insights are derived.
