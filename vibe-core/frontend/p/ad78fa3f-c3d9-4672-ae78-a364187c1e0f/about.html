Our Vision

To be the definitive source for environmental data in India, empowering informed decisions that drive sustainable development and protect our planet for future generations. We envision a future where data-driven insights lead to measurable improvements in air quality and a healthier India.

Data Integrity & Methodology

Trust is paramount when dealing with critical environmental data. Our platform is built on a foundation of data integrity and transparent methodologies. We meticulously source our data from reputable government agencies like the CPCB and MoEFCC, alongside globally recognized databases such as EDGAR and Climate Watch (WRI). Where direct real-time data is limited, we employ scientifically validated emission factors and models, adapting established methodologies like those from the IPCC to the Indian context.

Our Approach Includes:

  • Data Aggregation: Consolidating information from multiple official and research sources.
  • Emission Factor Database: Utilizing curated factors specific to Indian sources.
  • Advanced Analytics: Employing statistical models for trend analysis and insights.
  • Methodological Transparency: Clearly outlining data sources and calculation processes.
