Much Wow! What's All the Bark About?

Born in 2013 as a lighthearted joke, Dogecoin (DOGE) has rocketed from internet meme to a global phenomenon. Created by Billy Markus and Jackson Palmer, it was designed to be a fun, accessible alternative to the serious world of Bitcoin.

Powered by a Proof-of-Work (PoW) blockchain, Doge is known for its low transaction fees, quick confirmations, and a truly massive, energetic community. It’s the coin that proves you don’t need to be overly technical to be a part of the future of finance. Ready to join the pack?
