Our Journey to the Moon!

Follow our progress as we build the future of the people's crypto, one wagging tail at a time!

Q1 2023

Genesis Block & Community Launch

The foundation was laid! We officially launched the project, establishing our core values and kicking off our initial community-building efforts. Much hype, very wow!

Detailed community guidelines were established, early social channels were created, and our initial website went live. The core team bonded over memes and a shared vision.

Q2 2023

Meme Treasury & First Airdrop

We funded the official Meme Treasury and rewarded our early supporters with our first DOGE airdrop! Because sharing is caring, and memes are currency.

The Meme Treasury was established to fund community-driven content creation. The airdrop aimed to distribute DOGE widely, fostering early adoption and engagement.

Q3 2023

Community Project Incubator

Launched the Community Project Incubator to support innovative ideas within the Doge ecosystem. From charity drives to NFT projects, we're backing the pack!

Selected the first cohort of projects, providing mentorship, resources, and initial funding. Focus on projects that align with Doge's spirit of fun, charity, and accessibility.

Q4 2023

DogeFest & IRL Engagements

Hosted the first ever DogeFest! A celebration of all things Doge, bringing the community together for fun, learning, and maybe a few dog puns. Many IRL connections made!

Organized virtual and limited in-person meetups, fostering a stronger sense of community and providing opportunities for members to connect and share their Doge experiences.

Q1 2024

DogeChain Integration & Utility Push

Exploring seamless integration with the DogeChain ecosystem to unlock new utility and possibilities for DOGE holders. More ways to use your Doge!

Initiated partnerships and development efforts to enhance Dogecoin's utility, focusing on DeFi, NFTs, and real-world applications built on or interacting with the Doge network.

Ongoing

To Infinity & Beyond!

The journey never ends! We're continuously working on community growth, technological advancements, and spreading the Doge spirit across the globe. The moon is just the beginning!

Continuous development, community engagement initiatives, educational content creation, and exploration of new partnerships and use cases for Dogecoin.
