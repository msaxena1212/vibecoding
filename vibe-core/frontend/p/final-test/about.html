Our Story

Nova Horizon was founded on the belief that space travel should be accessible, sustainable, and transformative. We are dedicated to providing unparalleled experiences that inspire awe and wonder, while minimizing our environmental impact. Our team of experts brings together decades of experience in aerospace, luxury travel, and sustainable development to create a new era of space exploration.
